Hi, I'm Maya

I care most about people who don't get to choose their circumstances.

I've spent the last two summers at Global Refuge and The Sanctuary in Baltimore, working alongside families navigating systems that weren't built with them in mind. That work sits right next to basketball and rowing for me — not opposite it. Both are about showing up when it's hard and doing the unglamorous parts well.

I'm fifteen. I don't have this all figured out yet, and I'm not pretending to. This site is where I'm building the throughline in public, one season and one piece of writing at a time.
